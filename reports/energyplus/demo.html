Comfort is integrated at 15-minute resolution across all zones; the chart shows hourly temperatures for one zone. Lower cost with worse comfort is a tradeoff. Fixed-21 requests 21 °C all day. Scheduled requests 21 °C at 08:00–18:00 and 18 °C otherwise, with a narrow ±0.05 °C heating/cooling deadband: it can actively cool overnight and is not a conventional wide-deadband setback.

Scope matters. A four-day, one-seed pilot with a ten-second neural solver budget and four DFL epochs. This is neither annual savings nor a full reproduction of the paper tables. Gas is priced at an illustrative €0.10/kWh; PV, non-HVAC loads and fixed fees are excluded. Native warnings and all solver gaps remain in the report.
